Program Introduction

What began with two co-directors and six students in its inaugural 2009 cohort has since seen over 150 students who have gone on to have diverse and illustrious careers. Whether it's presenting at international conferences or hosting friendly soccer competitions, MOTSRTP trainees have done it all.

As you'll soon learn, being a trainee is a balancing act, between data collection and project management, competitions and event planning, and making sure to enjoy it along the way too!

Program Expectations

  1. Trainees are expected to be on site at least once a week! Many trainees commonly attend Transplant Rounds on Wednesdays and then stick around the office as Segun is often in office on Wednesday as well.
  2. Teams are expected to meet at least once a week. This ensures smooth communication as well as timely completion of tasks.
  3. Always communicate clearly with your team! If you aren't able to join a meeting or meet a deadline, make sure to let your team members know well ahead of time.
